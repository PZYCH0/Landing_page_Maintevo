Create & assign in seconds

Log the issue, describe what needs to be done, link it to the right asset, set a priority, and assign it to a technician. The whole thing takes under a minute. The technician gets notified immediately.

Tasks & checklists

Break each job into a list of steps. The technician works through them on-site and checks them off one by one. Nothing is left to interpretation — everyone knows what "done" looks like before they start.

Automatic time tracking

The technician starts a timer when they begin the job. It runs in the background and stops when they close it. Repair time is logged accurately — no estimates, no end-of-day memory exercises.

Close with a PDF report

When the job is done, close it with a summary note and generate a signed PDF report in one click. The report includes the job details, the technician's name, time spent, parts used, and the completed checklist.

How a work order moves through MaintEvo

Four stages, fully tracked at every step.

01
Reported

Someone spots a problem and logs it. The job is created with an asset link, a description, and a priority level.

02
Assigned

The manager reviews it, adds tasks and required parts, and assigns it to the right technician.

03
In Progress

The technician starts the timer, works through the checklist, and logs the parts they consume.

04
Closed

The job is completed, the time is recorded, and a PDF report is generated. The dashboard updates automatically.
