Parts catalog

Maintain a catalog of every spare part you stock. Each part has a name, reference number, storage location, quantity on hand, and minimum stock level. Finding what you need takes seconds.

Linked to work orders

When a technician uses parts during a job, they log them directly in the work order. Stock quantities update automatically. No separate spreadsheet, no manual inventory count at the end of the week.

Purchase orders

When stock falls below its minimum level, create a purchase order directly in MaintEvo. Track it from the moment it's submitted to the moment the parts arrive and are added back to stock.

Supplier management

Store supplier details — name, contact, lead time, pricing — alongside the parts they supply. When you need to reorder, everything is already there. No digging through emails or contacts.
