Everything your maintenance team needs

One platform to manage work orders, assets, preventive maintenance, inventory, and your whole team — from the office or the field.

Work Orders

Create, assign, prioritize, and close work orders in seconds. Track status in real time and maintain a full history for every task.

  • Drag-and-drop scheduling board
  • Photo & document attachments
  • Automated email & SMS notifications

Asset Management

Track every piece of equipment from purchase to retirement. Store specs, manuals, warranty info, and complete maintenance history in one place.

  • QR code & barcode scanning
  • Hierarchical asset trees
  • Depreciation & lifecycle tracking

Preventive Maintenance

Set up recurring maintenance schedules by date or usage threshold. Work orders are generated automatically, so no job is ever missed or created late.

  • Calendar & meter-based triggers
  • Auto-generated work orders
  • Compliance audit trails

Inventory & Parts

Know exactly what parts you have, where they are, and when to reorder. Link parts directly to work orders so technicians always have what they need.

  • Minimum stock level alerts
  • Multi-warehouse support
  • Purchase order integration

Reporting & Analytics

Make data-driven maintenance decisions with real-time dashboards and exportable reports on downtime, MTTR, costs, and technician performance.

  • Custom KPI dashboards
  • PDF & CSV exports
  • Scheduled automated reports

Mobile App

Technicians get full MaintEvo functionality on iOS and Android — including offline mode. Scan assets, log labor, upload photos, and close work orders from anywhere.

  • Offline-capable sync
  • Push notifications
  • Available on iOS & Android
