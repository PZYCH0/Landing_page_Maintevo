The 7 metrics, in plain language

All of these are calculated automatically. You don't touch any of them.

Repair Time
How long do repairs take?

The average time from when a job is opened to when it's closed. Tracked automatically using work order timers. If this number is going up, your team is getting slower — or jobs are getting harder.

Time Between Failures
How often does each asset fail?

The average time an asset runs between one failure and the next. A low number means frequent failures — a useful signal for replacement decisions.

Response Time
How fast does your team respond?

The time between when a problem is reported and when work actually starts. Useful for spotting bottlenecks in your approval or assignment process.

Availability
What percentage of the time is each asset actually running?

Calculated from repair time and time between failures. The higher this number, the more reliable your equipment is.

PM Compliance
Are planned jobs being done on time?

The percentage of scheduled preventive maintenance jobs that were completed within their target window. A direct measure of how well your PM program is being followed.

Downtime
How much time has each asset been out of service?

Cumulative downtime per asset, tracked from job open to job close. Useful for identifying which equipment is costing you the most productive time.

Failure Rate
How many failures per 1,000 hours of operation?

A normalized measure of how failure-prone an asset is over time. Useful when comparing equipment of different ages or types.
